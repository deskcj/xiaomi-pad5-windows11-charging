# Scope and attribution

The underlying Qualcomm Windows driver and its original copyright/resource notices remain attributable to their original owners. The user supplied the fix20 binary and archive for investigation. The fix20 author/source repository has not been independently identified; no claim to authorship of the original driver or fix20 is made.

New repair C source, constrained patcher, tests and installer were prepared for this investigation. Android source is referenced through Xiaomi's official repository and retains its own licenses; a complete Android kernel is not republished here.

Binary packages are experimental development/test artifacts. They are not official Xiaomi/Qualcomm support packages and have no WHQL or production-signing claim. Private keys, live machine backups, registry exports, memory dumps and firmware images are excluded from publication.
